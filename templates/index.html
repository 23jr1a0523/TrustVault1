📋

Immutable Audit Logs

Zero-Trust Security at Every Step

TrustVault strictly enforces role-based access control (RBAC). Document owners retain absolute control to share viewer permissions with specific users or revoke access instantly. Non-owners and unauthorized requests are denied access with explicit HTTP 403 enforcement and security incident logging.
